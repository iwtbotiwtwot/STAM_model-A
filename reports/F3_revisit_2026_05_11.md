## Synthesis — F3 and F3-cosmology are the same effective stress-energy seen two ways

The mathematical object is the same in both scripts: the effective T_μν that would source the Model-A metric under Einstein gravity. F3 looked at the spatial profile in a static spherically symmetric solution (ρ_eff(r), with r related to A by A = Rs/r). Script 34 looked at the cosmological equation of state by also computing the pressure components and forming w = p/ρ.

**The spatial-profile and equation-of-state views are mutually consistent:**
- F3's "negative ρ_eff at A < 0.44" = script 34's "w → -1 at low A" (dark-energy-like regime).
- F3's "positive ρ_eff at A > 0.44" = script 34's "w → 0 at high A" (matter-like regime).

**The cosmic-volume average depends critically on the assumed A profile:**

Script 34 used `A_cosmo(r) = (r/L)²`, which is the derived consequence of treating the cosmic A field as Poisson-sourced by critical-density matter inside a Hubble sphere centered on the observer. Under that profile, A grows from 0 at the observer to ~1 at the Hubble distance, and most volume sits at moderate-to-high A. Volume-weighted w drops to -0.49 because the high-A regions dominate.

But the framework's current cosmological commitment is *different*: A_0 = 1/(12π) is the cosmic floor (a constant), and structure-dependent A_LoS modulates around it. The (r/L)² profile is one specific model of cosmic A — not the only one, and not the current commitment.

**Under the current commitment (cosmic A ≈ A_0, nearly constant):**
- The relevant w is the *local* w at A ≈ A_0 ≈ 0.0265.
- From script 34's table, w(A=0.0099) = -0.978. Extrapolating to A_0 = 0.0265 (between A=0.01 and A=0.09), w ≈ -0.94 to -0.95.

**So the original "NOT dark-energy-like" verdict of script 34 was an artifact of the assumed cosmic profile, not a property of the Model-A metric.** With the post-G7 cosmic profile, the prediction flips: Model-A's effective stress-energy at the cosmic floor IS approximately a cosmological constant.
