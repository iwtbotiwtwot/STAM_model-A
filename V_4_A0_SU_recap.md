## Where "A_0 = 1/SU" fits

This formulation appeared during the derivation work. It's correct when SU is read as a **number** rather than as a ruler tick:

- If **SU (the number) = 12π** (the Planck-cell conversion factor), then **A_0 = 1/SU = 1/(12π)** ✓

The primary, cleanest formulation is **1 SU = A_0**. The `A_0 = 1/SU` form is useful when you want to emphasize that A_0 takes the inverse-numerical form of the Planck-cell conversion factor.
