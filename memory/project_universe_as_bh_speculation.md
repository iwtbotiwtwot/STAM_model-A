**Off-the-record vision** shared by Sean 2026-05-11. Speculative parking-lot, **not a current framework commitment**. Captured for continuity so future sessions can engage with the picture without re-deriving it.

## The vision

The universe might be structurally similar to a black hole, viewed from the inside. Under this reading:
- We live "inside" what is structurally analogous to a BH's interior (which Model-A says is the boundary's hologram, since no real BH interior exists).
- The cosmic horizon would be the outer face of this structure.
- **Dark energy** would be matter accumulating on the outward surface of the cosmic horizon — accretion onto the universe-as-BH from outside.
- This outward accumulation **increases the horizon size without adding mass to the observable hologram** that constitutes the inside-of-the-universe.
- The universe would "stretch" (horizon grows) without the contents of the observable hologram getting heavier.

## Why this is captured but flagged as speculative

- **Off-the-record.** Sean shared this as a vision he's exploring, explicitly off-record. He hasn't committed to it as framework content.
- **No mathematical development yet.** The picture is conceptual. Quantitative predictions, modified Friedmann equations under this reading, comparison to BAO/SN/CMB data — none of this exists.
- **It would substantially refactor the cosmological branch.** The current cosmological commitment is "matter-only EdS universe; dark energy is remitted as photon-A traversal misinterpretation" (project_cosmological_commitment.md). The universe-as-BH reading would replace that with "dark energy as outer-surface accretion onto the cosmic BH." These are different mechanisms. Adopting the new picture would require revisiting G7's bridge term derivation and the F3-cosmology effective stress-energy reading.
