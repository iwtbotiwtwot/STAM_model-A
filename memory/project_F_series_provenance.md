The F1-F6 series of scripts ("fatalities") were Claude-generated as adversarial tests intended to identify ways STAM might be falsified by standard physics objections. Author clarified 2026-05-11: these were not his ideas; he OK'd the series but was not heavily invested in understanding them at the equation level at the time.

The series:
- **F1 Penrose singularity evasion** — bold-STAM avoids closed trapped surfaces, ergo Penrose's theorem doesn't apply.
- **F3 Birkhoff theorem evasion** — bold-STAM isn't vacuum (A field is a physical field), ergo Birkhoff's premise unmet. Verdict: "PARTIALLY SURVIVED — owes a specification of field equations."
- **F5 GW polarization** — pure scalar gravity gives only longitudinal/breathing modes, ruled out by LIGO.
- **F5b GW polarization Model-B** — Model-B's "GWs are space, light is matter on space" fix; GWs inherit tensor character from metric perturbations.
- **F5c GW170817 arrival test** — the asymmetric-propagation half of Model-B (GW = c, light slowed by ∫A) falsified at ~3 years vs observed 1.74 s.
- **F6 gravitational decoherence** — predicted τ ≈ 0.5 s for 1 µm silica, scales as m^(5/3). Frontier-testable.

**What survived F-series, in the current commitment:**
- F5's polarization issue met by treating GWs as metric perturbations h_μν (tensor character inherited from being perturbations of g_μν), not as A perturbations. The surviving piece of the Model-B reading.
- F5c dropped asymmetric propagation entirely. The 1.74 s GW170817 delay is now read as astrophysical jet-launch timing, not propagation difference.
- F1 verdict still stands: Penrose's theorem premise unmet.
- F3 verdict "partially survived" is the author's flagged item for re-examination 2026-05-11 (see priority for that session).
- F6 stands as a frontier prediction.

The latter are commitments he owns and can speak to directly; the former are Claude-proposed tests whose verdicts he provisionally accepted.
