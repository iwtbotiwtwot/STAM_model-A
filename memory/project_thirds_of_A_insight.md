**Author's intuition (2026-05-07):** The shadow / ISCO / photon sphere should fall at thirds of A.

**Confirmed by direct calculation:**

| Feature | r in Rs units | A = Rs/r |
|---|---|---|
| ISCO (innermost stable circular orbit) | 3 Rs | **1/3** |
| Photon sphere (unstable circular photon orbit) | 1.5 Rs | **2/3** |
| Event horizon | Rs | **3/3 = 1** |

This is not a coincidence. The condition for the photon sphere is `2h(A) = r × dh/dr`, which gives r = 1.5 Rs ↔ A = 2/3 for h = 1-A. The ISCO comes from the marginal-stability condition `d²/dr² (effective potential) = 0`, which for Schwarzschild gives r = 3 Rs ↔ A = 1/3. The horizon is where h = 0 ↔ A = 1.

**Why Model-A preserves all three exactly:**

The circular-orbit conditions and stability conditions depend on `h(A)` (the g_tt coefficient) but NOT on `k(A)` (the g_rr coefficient). The k coefficient enters only as a multiplicative kinetic factor that doesn't change zero-radial-velocity locations or stability roots.

Since Model-A has `g_tt = -(1-A) c² = GR exactly`, the special radii are unchanged:
- ISCO at A = 1/3 ✓ (accretion disk inner edge — matches GR)
- Photon sphere at A = 2/3 ✓ (EHT shadow size — matches GR)
- Event horizon at A = 1 ✓ (same boundary location)

**Consequence: even stronger GR-exterior recovery than SF4 suggested.**

The most-measured GR strong-field predictions — EHT shadow size and accretion disk inner edge — depend purely on h(A). Model-A matches both exactly. Where Model-A differs is in:
- Radial Shapiro timing near the photon sphere (depends on √(hk))
- Quasinormal mode ringdown frequencies (depends on full near-horizon geometry)
- Tidal disruption physics very close to horizon

These are subtler observables that current instruments don't constrain tightly. EHT shadow size — the obvious "GR confirmation" attack — does NOT distinguish Model-A from GR.

**How to apply:**
- When discussing strong-field tests, distinguish carefully between observables that depend on h alone (preserved exactly) vs observables that depend on h and k together (where Model-A differs).
- EHT shadow size: Model-A = GR. Don't claim EHT testability.
- Accretion disk inner edge: Model-A = GR. Don't claim ISCO testability.
- Near-horizon Shapiro / QNM ringdown: Model-A ≠ GR. These are the genuine falsifiable corners.
- The "thirds of A" pattern is a clean STAM-language statement of GR's geometric structure. Use it in any explanatory writeup.
