**Quantum interpretation working analogy (author's words, 2026-05-07):**

A measurement is a physical interaction that commits the path; once committed, a classical path is set. The blockchain framing carries: append-only commit, irreversibility, distributed/local rather than central, ordered.

The horizon connects the two branches: `A=1` is the point where `S_h(A)` diverges *and* where external A-reconciliation becomes impossible (`A>1` → "unresolved relative to outside"). Hawking-like radiation would be the mechanism by which interior A eventually reconciles to the external ledger.
