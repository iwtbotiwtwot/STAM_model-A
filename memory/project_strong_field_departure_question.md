Author flagged 2026-05-11 as a priority structural question worth writing down: **what makes Model-A's strong-field departure from GR concrete?**

Currently the strong-field commitment (project_strong_field_commitment.md) is:
- g_tt = -(1-A) c² (identical to GR, ensures weak-field tests pass automatically)
- g_rr = 1 / [(1-A)(1-A²)²] (the k(A) = (1-A)(1-A²)² choice — STAM-derived in its consequences, postulated in its origin)

The k(A) form is what gives Model-A its falsifiable strong-field content:
- "Falls forever" at A=1 (infinite proper time + SU divergence)
- G1's QNM damping factor τ_MA/τ_GR ≈ 1.80 (in tension with current LIGO data, eikonal approximation)
- The third "3" in G17 (Schwarzschild-coordinate g_rr ratio 12:4)
- F1 Penrose evasion via no strictly trapped surfaces
- F3 effective stress-energy giving native dark-energy-like w ≈ -0.978

But k(A) itself is not derived from a Lagrangian or first-principles symmetry argument. The field-equations gap (project_field_equations_gap.md) is the same gap viewed from a different angle: no action specifying that this k(A) is what self-consistency requires.

**Why this matters:**
- If a future LIGO QNM measurement contradicts τ_MA/τ_GR ≈ 1.80, the response is either (a) k(A) was wrong, in which case much of the strong-field corpus needs revision, or (b) Model-A is falsified at the strong-field metric level.
- Without a derivation that picks k(A) = (1-A)(1-A²)² out of the space of possible scalar-tensor metric constructions, Model-A's strong-field departure is structurally arbitrary — it has the right shape for the no-crossing intuition, but its specific functional form is a choice.
- Answering this question — what feature of the framework forces this k(A) — would convert k(A) from postulate to derivation, and convert the strong-field corpus from "consequences of a chosen k(A)" to "consequences of Model-A's structure."

**How to apply:**
- When designing strong-field tests, remember that the falsification target is k(A), not Model-A's deeper commitments. A test failing k(A) does not necessarily falsify Model-A's foundational A-field picture.
- When proposing structural arguments (the third 3 in G17, the (4π × 3) decomposition, the standing-mode derivation of thirds), keep watching for what features ride uniquely on k(A) vs what features ride on more general Model-A structure.
- Concrete derivations that would close this question:
  1. Lagrangian for A (scalar-tensor or non-canonical) that uniquely produces this k(A) under self-consistency. Script 41 attempted this for V_1; never finished for V_3 era.
  2. Variational principle from boundary ontology — the no-interior commitment + symmetric-boundary commitment may pick out k(A) via an obstacle-problem-style argument.
  3. Standing-mode wave equation on (A_0, 1) — if a specific wave equation reproduces the orbital thirds, the kinetic-term structure that does so may force the metric structure.

This is filed as ongoing priority. Not a single-session task; the answer might come from one of the routes above, from a route not yet imagined, or from data forcing the issue (e.g., exact Regge-Wheeler vs LIGO).
